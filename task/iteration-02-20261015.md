## 第二轮提示词：英文提交，中文用于理解

### 雨声

```text
A continuous, even bed of gentle rain falling on the glass roof of a small greenhouse, heard softly from indoors. Steady rainfall intensity and consistent volume throughout, with fine soft droplets blending into a smooth background texture. Seamless ambient loop with no beginning or ending swell. No thunder, wind gusts, sudden impacts, voices, or music.
```

中文对照：从室内听见细雨落在小型温室的玻璃屋顶上，形成连续、均匀、柔和的雨声底层。全程雨势稳定、音量一致，细小轻柔的雨滴融合成平滑的背景声。首尾无明显接缝地循环，不在开头或结尾渐强。不要雷声、阵风、突然的撞击声、人声或音乐。

### 木板敲击

```text
One very light tap on a small wooden board. A soft, warm, muted woody tone with a gentle rounded attack and a short natural resonance. Light and delicate, with no heavy thud or sharp click. Isolated single tap, close dry recording, no background sounds or music.
```

中文对照：非常轻地敲一下小木板。声音柔和、温暖，带有收敛的木质音色，起音轻柔圆润，共鸣短而自然。整体轻巧细腻，不要沉重的闷击或尖锐的咔嗒声。只要独立的一次敲击，近距离干声录制，不带背景声音或音乐。

### 种子发芽

```text
A single soft, continuous organic unfurling sound for a tiny seed sprouting. Delicate leaf-like rustling with a smooth gentle onset, an even soft body, and a short natural fade. Quiet, warm, and close throughout. No initial click, snap, crack, pop, sharp transient, magical chime, voices, or music.
```

中文对照：为小种子发芽制作一次柔和、连续、带自然质感的舒展声。像嫩叶一样细微地窸窣，起音平滑轻柔，主体均匀柔和，最后短暂自然淡出。全程安静、温暖、贴近。不要开头的咔嗒、脆响、裂响、爆音或尖锐起音，不要魔法钟声、人声或音乐。
